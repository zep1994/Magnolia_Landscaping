A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Let’s clear
things up.

Do you work with homes and businesses?+

Yes. You can request residential landscape and lawn care or commercial grounds maintenance. We’ll tailor the scope to your property.

How much will my landscaping cost?+

Every property is different. Share your goals, property size, and the services you need, and we’ll discuss a custom quote before any work begins.

Can I schedule recurring lawn care?+

Yes. Essential and complete landscape care plans are designed for recurring service. Frequency and seasonal needs are discussed during your consultation.

Do you serve my neighborhood?+

Include your city and ZIP code in the consultation form so we can confirm service availability for your location.

What happens after I submit a request?+

Once email delivery is configured, your request is sent to the Magnolia team. We’ll follow up to discuss your property, timing, and next steps.
