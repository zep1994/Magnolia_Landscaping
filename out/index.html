GOOD QUESTIONS. STRAIGHT ANSWERS.

A few things
you might be wondering.

Can I book several small repairs together?+

Absolutely. Add your entire punch list to the request. We’ll discuss what can be grouped into a visit and provide an estimate for the agreed work.

How much will my project cost?+

Pricing depends on the work, materials, and time involved. Share the details so we can discuss a clear estimate before you commit.

Is my requested date a confirmed appointment?+

No. Your preferred date helps us plan, but appointments are confirmed only after we discuss the project, approve the scope, and agree on availability.

Do you handle electrical, plumbing, or structural work?+

Tell us what you need. Work requiring specialized licensing, permits, or structural expertise should be handled by the appropriate qualified professional. We’ll discuss whether your project fits our services.

Do you serve my area?+

Include your city and ZIP code in your request so service availability can be confirmed.

What should I do before a visit?+

Clear access to the work area and mention any relevant property rules or material preferences when your appointment is arranged.
