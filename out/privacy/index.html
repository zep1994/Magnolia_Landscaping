Privacy policy

When you request a consultation, we collect the name, email address, phone number, location, service preferences, and project details you provide.

How we use your information

We use this information to respond to your request, discuss services, and prepare your consultation. We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers

This website is hosted on Cloudflare. Consultation requests are delivered through Resend when email delivery is configured. These providers process information necessary to operate the website and deliver your request.

Cookies and external images

This website does not use advertising cookies or analytics trackers. Photos load from Unsplash, which receives standard connection information such as your IP address when an image is requested.

Your choices

Only share information needed for your landscaping request. To ask about your information or request its deletion, submit a consultation request mentioning your privacy question or reply to a previous email from our team.

Updates

This policy may be updated as website services change. Last updated October 9, 2026.
