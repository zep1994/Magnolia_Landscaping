Privacy policy

This is a demonstration website for Red Clay Home Services. Use fictional information when trying the booking flow.

Demo requests

The booking form validates your entries and displays a sample confirmation. Your form information is held only in the page’s memory. It is not sent to a server, stored in a database, or delivered by email. Reloading the page clears your demo request.

External services

The website is intended for Cloudflare hosting. Stock photography loads from Unsplash. Hosting and image providers may process standard connection information, such as your IP address, when serving content.

Phone links

The telephone number shown is fictional. Clicking a phone link may open your device’s dialer; it does not connect you to a real Red Clay business line.

Cookies

This site does not use advertising cookies or analytics trackers.

Future live deployment

Before accepting real customer requests, the business should replace this policy with its actual contact information, data handling practices, and retention policy.
